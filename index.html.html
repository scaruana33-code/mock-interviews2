⭐ STAR structure — build your answer on this skeleton
S
Situation — set the scene in 1–2 sentences (where, when, your role, the stakes).
T
Task — the specific problem you owned. Make ownership explicit ("I was responsible for…").
A
Action — 60% of your answer. The steps you personally took. Use "I", not "we". Show the data.
R
Result — quantify it (%, $, time, SLA, adoption). Add what you learned or scaled afterward.
