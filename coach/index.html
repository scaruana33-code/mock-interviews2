Prediction Engine & Post-Interview Refresh

Track each application through its stages. Rose estimates the odds of clearing each next gate, then updates them after every debrief.

How the odds work: they start from public hiring-funnel rates (about 75% of résumés filtered by ATS; roughly 3% of applicants reach an interview), then adjust for your fit score, referral, practice scores and debrief signals. The adjustments are hand-set estimates until your own outcomes recalibrate them in the Learning loop tab. They are not a guarantee.
